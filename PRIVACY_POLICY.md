# Privacy Policy (Draft)

This is a draft privacy policy for WarrantyVault. Update the text below to match your actual data practices before publishing.

1. Data Collected
- WarrantyVault stores warranty items and attachments (receipts, photos) locally on the user's device.
- Optional account metadata (name, email) is stored locally in encrypted preferences.

2. Use of Data
- Data is used solely to provide the app's features: storing items, scheduling reminders, and exporting/importing backups.

3. Sharing
- The app does not share user data with third parties by default.

4. Backups & Export
- Users can export their data to local files (JSON/ZIP) which may include attachments. Users are responsible for where they store exports.

5. Security
- Local data is stored in a Room database and attachments are stored under app-private storage. Sensitive preferences are encrypted with AndroidX Security.

- Update with contact email and link to full privacy policy on your website.
